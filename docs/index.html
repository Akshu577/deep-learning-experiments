Deep Learning & Generative Models: Experiments

These are six experiments from my MS in Artificial Intelligence (University of the Cumberlands). Each one starts from the reference code in Generative Deep Learning, 2nd ed. (David Foster, O'Reilly; code on GitHub, Apache 2.0). I then changed one thing (an activation function, the stride, the training length, the sampling temperature) and measured what happened. The notebooks say exactly what I changed from the book.

Built with TensorFlow / Keras 3.

# Experiment Data What I changed and what I found
01 MLP activation functions Fashion-MNIST Same network with ReLU, LeakyReLU and Sigmoid. Test accuracy 88.0% / 88.7% / 88.3%. On a network this shallow, the choice barely matters.
02 CNN stride CIFAR-10 Stride 2 vs. stride 3. Accuracy dropped from 72.8% to 65.5%, because the larger stride throws away spatial detail too early.
03 Autoencoder Fashion-MNIST Trained a convolutional autoencoder and studied where reconstructions lose detail.
04 LSTM text generation Epicurious recipes Trained a word-level LSTM (loss 1.52 after 25 epochs) and compared generated recipes at temperature 0.5 vs. 0.8 (sample output).
05 RealNVP normalizing flow Two Moons Trained twice as long as the book (600 epochs). Loss went 2.56 → 1.70 and the flow maps the moons to a Gaussian and back.
06 Energy-based model MNIST Trained for 120 epochs instead of 60. Training collapsed after epoch 80, and the notebook analyses why using the Langevin samples and the replay buffer.

The most interesting one is 06. Training longer made the model worse, and figuring out why taught me more than the runs that worked.
